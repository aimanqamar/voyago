Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I book a flight?

Choose your cities, date and travellers, press Search flights, pick a flight, enter traveller details and pay. Your e-ticket appears instantly.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancel more than 72 hours before departure for a full refund, or between 24 and 72 hours for a 70% refund. Open Manage booking at the top and enter your reference and email.

What do I need to bring?

A valid passport or ID matching the name you entered, and your booking reference (like VG-AB12CD). You can print your ticket from the confirmation screen.

Is my card information safe?

We only keep the card brand and last four digits. Your full card number is never saved.

Are there any discounts?

Yes — enter WELCOME10 for 10% off, or FAMILY15 for 15% off when booking for three or more travellers.

Is this a real airline?

No. Voyago is a demo project: payments are simulated and no real tickets are issued or charged.
